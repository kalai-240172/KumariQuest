🛡️ Safety & Travel Tips

Useful information to help you enjoy a safe and comfortable trip around Kanyakumari.

🚶 General Safety

  • Keep your important belongings safe.
  • Avoid isolated places during late hours.
  • Keep emergency contact numbers available.
  • Follow local rules and instructions.

🌊 Beach Safety

  • Follow safety instructions near the sea.
  • Avoid entering restricted areas.
  • Be careful during strong waves.
  • Stay with your group.

☀️ Weather Tips

  • Carry drinking water.
  • Use sunscreen during daytime travel.
  • Carry an umbrella when required.
  • Check weather conditions before outdoor trips.

📱 Emergency

  • Keep your phone charged.
  • Save important emergency contacts.
  • Contact local authorities when necessary.
  • Share your travel plan with someone you trust.
